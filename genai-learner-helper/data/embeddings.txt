Embeddings are numerical vector representations of text.

They convert words, sentences, or documents into dense vectors in high-dimensional space.

Embeddings allow semantic similarity comparison between texts.

For example:
- "What is GenAI?" and "Explain Generative AI" will have similar embeddings.

In a RAG system:

1. Documents are converted into embeddings.
2. User query is also converted into an embedding.
3. Vector similarity search finds the most relevant documents.

Popular embedding models:
- sentence-transformers/all-MiniLM-L6-v2
- OpenAI text-embedding models
- BGE embeddings
- Instructor embeddings

Embedding similarity is usually measured using:
- Cosine similarity
- Dot product
- Euclidean distance

Embeddings are stored in vector databases like FAISS for efficient retrieval.
