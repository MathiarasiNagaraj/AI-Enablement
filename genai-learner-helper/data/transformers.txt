The Transformer architecture was introduced in 2017 in the research paper "Attention Is All You Need" by Vaswani et al.

Transformers replaced traditional Recurrent Neural Networks (RNNs) and LSTMs in many NLP tasks.

Key components of Transformer architecture:

1. Self-Attention Mechanism:
   Allows the model to focus on different parts of the input sequence when processing each token.

2. Multi-Head Attention:
   Multiple attention heads run in parallel to capture different relationships in the data.

3. Positional Encoding:
   Since Transformers process tokens in parallel, positional encoding is added to preserve word order information.

4. Feed-Forward Neural Network:
   Applied after attention layers to introduce non-linearity.

Transformers are the foundation of modern Large Language Models (LLMs) such as:
- GPT models
- BERT
- T5
- LLaMA
- Mistral

Advantages of Transformers:
- Parallel processing
- Better long-range dependency handling
- Scalable training
